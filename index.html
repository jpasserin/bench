Find broken photo links

Scans people and activities in small chunks — the first 5 photos of every record first, then deeper only if that quick pass finds nothing. When a photo fails to load, the record gets a Broken Images meta tag so you can find and fix it. Stops after finding 5 people and 5 activities.

Find low-quality photos

Scans people and activities for records whose best photo is still below the low-quality threshold (short side under px — set above). Those records get a Low Quality meta tag. Stops after finding 5 people and 5 activities.

Repair corrupted dates

Some dates may have been saved in a raw form like Mon Apr 26 2004 00:00:00 GMT-0700. This one-time fix scans every person birth date, activity date and seen-week and reformats them to YYYY-MM-DD (filling in the year only where it's missing). You'll be shown a count and asked to confirm.

Find duplicate people

Groups people who look like the same person — comparing each person's name and alias (case, accents and punctuation ignored, word order too). A match on a name ↔ alias counts. Pick the primary to keep, tick who to merge in, and Merge combines everything (activities, ratings, awards, teams, photos, tags…) — you'll be asked to resolve any conflicting values first.

Photo rotation

How fast photos auto-advance in the Challenge, Rate and Tag Me pages (milliseconds — lower is faster).

Low-quality photo threshold

In the photo picker, an image is flagged ⚠️ low quality when its short side is under this many pixels. One value covers both portrait and landscape. Lower it to flag fewer images.

Rating-change arrows

The ▲/▼ arrows (profile bubbles, leaderboard, Trending) only count rating changes within this many days.

Rating approval

A newly rated skill is provisional (shows a ?) until it has been compared this many times against established ratings — or you approve it by hand (tap the rating bubble on a profile). While provisional, a rating doesn't move other people's ratings when compared and doesn't count as a recent ▲/▼ change. Set to 0 to disable (every new rating counts immediately).
